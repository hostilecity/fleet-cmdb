VT-D01 review. The override flow: an operator records that a compensating control is in place, or accepts a risk for a while. It is its own page, like the Phase 1 host actions, reached from "Record a control…" or "Accept the risk…" on the Needs attention section. Four states are drawn: the form, the form with errors, the saved result with its audit entry, and a suspended decision.

Applies to

Audit entries, one action each: vulnerability.decision_recorded, vulnerability.decision_changed, vulnerability.decision_ended (by an operator), vulnerability.decision_expired and vulnerability.decision_suspended (by the system, with the evidence that changed). A decision never changes a finding's state or severity, and it never removes the finding from any open count: the totals stay honest, only the bucket moves. Whether saving needs the second factor is review question V10.
