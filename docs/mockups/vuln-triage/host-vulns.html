Suggested compensating controls

  • Network service, no login needed (smbd, sshd): limit who can reach the port with the host firewall (this host's firewall is enabled, nftables), bind the service to the LAN interface only, or stop it if unused.
  • Known exploited: treat as urgent. Check the vendor advisory for a mitigation, watch the service's logs, and consider taking the service off the network until Debian ships a fix.
  • Crafted files (image and video decoders): process untrusted files in a sandboxed service account, or turn off automatic thumbnailing.
  • Unsupported package (end-of-life): plan a replacement; until then keep it away from untrusted input.
  • Loaded by a listening service (ssh.service, N5): stop the service if nothing needs it, bind it to localhost or a private interface, and allow only the hosts that need it in the firewall.

Suggestions come from a short table in the repository keyed by rule and attack vector (VT-S05), never from a third party. They are advice; nothing is changed on the host. A recorded control moves a finding to Low risk; an accepted risk does not.
