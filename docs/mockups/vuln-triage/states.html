Second, missing data never makes a finding look safer or lowers a score; the page says what is missing instead.

EPSS works the same way: the last stored bands keep being used past its age limit (7 days by default), and nothing is rewritten when it goes stale or recovers. Turning a feed off in the configuration keeps its last data, which then shows as stale. It never lowers a score.
