Known exploited (CISA KEV) doubles an advisory's points, except in Ignorable. An advisory counts once per host, in its worst bucket, however many binary packages it matches (openssl and libssl3t64 are one advisory, not two). The table is a published constant in the spec, not a model, and the same everywhere.
