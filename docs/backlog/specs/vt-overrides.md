# VT-OVERRIDES: Operator decisions: record a control or accept a risk, with expiry, reason, and audit

Implementation details for the GitHub issue with this key. The issue states only the goal and acceptance criteria; this file is the source of truth for everything else. Goal, requirement IDs, acceptance criteria, and blockers live in [`../issues.yml`](../issues.yml).

Part of [VT-EPIC](vt-epic.md) (approved by the owner, 2026-11-19); decided in [ADR 0032](../../decisions/0032-vulnerability-triage-buckets.md). ADR 0022 left "per-finding risk acceptance" for a later decision; this is it. Drawn in [override.html](../../mockups/vuln-triage/override.html).

## VT-S06: Decisions

### Scope

- Table `finding_decisions`: host, advisory, kind (`control`, `accepted`), reason (10 to 1,000 characters, sanitised), expires on (a date), created by and at, ended by, at, and why (`operator`, `expired`, `fixed`, `suspended`, `resolved`), and the evidence at creation (bucket and rule, severity, KEV, EPSS band, attack vector, listening class). One active decision per host and advisory, covering every binary package the advisory matches on the host. A decision is never deleted.
- Allowed only on findings in Needs attention or Low risk. Expiry at most `FLEET_DECISION_MAX_DAYS` (90), or `FLEET_DECISION_MAX_DAYS_KEV` (30) for a KEV-listed advisory (epic decision 15).
- **Effect** (S1, epic decision 12):
  - **Control in place** (rule O1) moves the finding to Low risk, scored as "Low risk by a control" (10, 5, 2, 1 points; KEV doubled), well above plain Low risk. A KEV-listed finding under a control keeps the "Known exploited" tier.
  - **Risk accepted** changes no bucket, points, or tier. The finding stays where the rules put it (normally Needs attention), marked "risk accepted until ...", and leaves "decisions due". Accepting a risk changes nothing about exposure, so it never lowers the score.
  - Neither changes the finding's state or severity, or removes it from the open count.
- **Ends by itself**: at expiry (a job every hour), when a fix appears (rules K1, K2, F1, F2), or when every finding it covers resolves. **Suspended** by the triage run when the evidence gets worse than recorded: a CVE added to KEV, the EPSS band becoming high, or the severity rising. A suspended control stops applying (the finding falls through to N1 to N4); a suspended acceptance returns the finding to "decisions due". Both stay visible with why.
- **Several hosts** (N8): one action may cover several hosts with the same open finding, but each host shows its own evidence, and a control must be confirmed host by host (a checkbox per host saying the control exists there). An acceptance may cover them together. Each host gets its own decision and audit entry, with a shared request id.
- **Audit**, one action each (FR-S.3): `vulnerability.decision_recorded`, `vulnerability.decision_changed`, `vulnerability.decision_ended` (operator), `vulnerability.decision_expired`, `vulnerability.decision_suspended` (system, with the evidence that changed).
- **Re-classify** the hosts concerned in their transitions after each change (VT-S05).
- Second factor (epic decision 37, replacing decision 14): required once an operator has recorded more than 10 host decisions within 15 minutes, in any session, counted on the server, and every time on a KEV-listed advisory; the existing step-up (P3-S09).
- Adds the decision links and tags to the host tab (VT-D04).

### Acceptance criteria

- [x] A control moves the finding to Low risk with its reason and expiry shown and its points as "by a control"; an acceptance keeps the finding's bucket, points, and tier; both are audited with the evidence and leave the open count unchanged (`spec/services/finding_decisions_spec.rb`, `spec/requests/finding_decisions_spec.rb`; the points on the summaries are VT-SCORES', which read the stored O1)
- [x] A decision ends at expiry and when a fix appears, and is suspended when its CVE is added to KEV, each audited by the system (`spec/services/finding_decisions_spec.rb`)
- [x] A decision longer than the limit, without a reason, on a Fix available or Ignorable finding, or a control not confirmed for a host, is refused with errors in place (both specs)

## VT-D05: The flow

### Scope

- A page per decision, as the Phase 1 host actions: kind, reason, until, the hosts it applies to (each with its evidence and, for a control, a confirmation), errors in place, CSRF-protected POST; the saved notice with the score's change (or "no change" for an acceptance); change or end from the finding row.
- The Risk page's "Decisions due" lists suspended decisions, decisions expiring within 7 days, and Needs attention advisories with no decision (accepted ones are not "due").

### Acceptance criteria

- [x] The form, errors, saved, and suspended states render as drawn, with labels and errors announced (`spec/requests/finding_decisions_spec.rb`; axe-core in As built)
- [x] Every value an operator typed is escaped wherever it is shown, including the audit log (the same spec)

## As built (#261)

- **Table** `finding_decisions` (`20261123261001`): host, advisory, kind, reason (sanitised, then 10 to 1,000 characters; a check constraint too), `expires_on` (the last day it applies, UTC), author, `confirmed` (a control must be), the evidence at creation (bucket and rule, severity, KEV and its CVEs, EPSS band and score, CVSS vector, listening class, firewall), the request id and operator session, and the end (`ended_at`, `ended_by` for an operator's end only, `ended_why`, and for a suspension `ended_evidence`: what got worse, recorded and now). A partial unique index allows one active decision per host and advisory. `finding_decisions_guard` refuses deletes and truncation, any change to an ended decision, and any change to what a decision is (host, advisory, kind, author, evidence, confirmation); only its reason, last day, and end may change. A check constraint keeps the last day within 366 days of the day it was recorded, even written past the application. The table's guard is owned by `cmdb`, so the audit log, not this table, is the record (threat model C18).
- **Effect.** `FindingDecisions::ControlSource` is #277's `control_source`: the controls in force (active and `expires_on` today or later) of a batch's hosts, by the partial index. O1 then applies as #277 built it (over the rules after O1, so never over a fix or an Ignorable rule). An acceptance changes no bucket: it only takes the advisory out of decisions due. A KEV finding under a control keeps its stored `kev` flag (VT-SCORES' tier reads it), and the tab says how many findings under a control are known exploited, in Needs attention (which is open by default) and on the O1 row of Low risk, so a control never hides one.
- **Recording** (`FindingDecisions.record!`). On every chosen host, the advisory's open findings must include one in Needs attention or Low risk and none in Fix available or not triaged yet (a kernel advisory also has Ignorable findings on its headers and old images, which O1 never moves). Spec gap, decided conservatively: a host with any Fix available finding of the advisory is refused ("upgrade instead"). The limit is 90 days, or 30 when any CVE of the advisory is on CISA's KEV list now. Each host is checked again under its row lock (taken in id order, as the transitions take them), then each gets its decision and audit entry with the request's id, and the advisory's findings on those hosts are re-triaged in the same transaction (`VulnerabilityMatcher.retriage!`, about 1 ms a host), so the saved notice reads the new bucket back rather than promising it. At most 50 hosts in one action. **Second factor** (epic decision 37): once the operator has recorded more than 10 host decisions within 15 minutes, in any of their sessions (`created_by_id`, counted again under the operator's row lock, so two requests at once cannot each stay under it, and neither splitting an action nor signing in again avoids it; `operator_session_id` is kept for the record), and a new code every time on a KEV-listed advisory, to record or to change: the save needs a code checked in that request, not an open window (threat model H12). The KEV status is read again under the locks: an advisory listed between the form and the save is refused, so its 30-day limit holds. The form lists this host and up to 49 others with the same open finding, from at most 3,920 of the advisory's finding rows (its index, no sort), so the rows read are bounded whatever the fleet size; when most have resolved, fewer hosts may be listed. Line breaks in a reason are made spaces, with the control and format characters.
- **Changing and ending.** A change sets a new reason or last day, never past the limit counted from the day the decision was recorded (decided conservatively: a decision cannot be renewed for ever on old evidence; a longer one is a new decision on today's evidence). Its kind cannot change (end it and record the other). An operator's end re-triages at once, and its audit entry reads the bucket back after the re-triage. A decision past its last day that the hourly job has not ended yet is never changed or ended by an operator: either action expires it there and then (`decision_expired`, by the system) and says so, so it cannot be revived. Likewise a change to a decision whose evidence got worse since it was recorded suspends it there and then (`decision_suspended`), rather than saving the change.
- **Ending by itself** (`FindingDecisions::Lifecycle`). In every host's transitions and triage re-runs, under the batch's locks and before the controls are read: decisions past their last day expire and those whose evidence got worse are suspended (a CVE of the advisory added to KEV, compared as lists of CVEs against the evidence, so a second CVE joining the list suspends too; the EPSS band turning high; the severity rising; an `unknown` severity counts as medium, as N4 counts it). After the findings are written: decisions whose findings got a fix (K1, K2, F1, F2) or all resolved end, audited as `vulnerability.decision_ended` by the system with why. `FindingDecisionExpiryJob` (hourly, minute 7) sweeps every active decision for expiry and worse evidence, a batch of 5,000 and their advisories' signals at a time through a partial index on the active rows (ended ones are kept for ever), keeping only each due decision's host and advisory, grouped by host, and for each slice of at most 100 hosts and 100 due decisions (a host with more is split by advisory) reviews them under the hosts' locks and re-triages the advisories concerned (linear in the decisions, review of #299); it catches what no re-triage reaches (an EPSS band turning high on an advisory already in N1 moves no bucket, so no run visits the host). A worse attack vector or new listener evidence does not suspend (the spec names three signals); the rules themselves still escalate a finding whose control is not in force.
- **Runs that are retried or resumed** (#292: `EvaluationRun.retry!` and `EvaluationRunWatchJob` cover every run kind). A triage or advisory run's batch that runs again re-runs the hosts' transitions, whose decision checks act on active decisions only and commit with the hosts' findings in one transaction a batch: nothing is ended or audited twice, and a batch killed part way leaves each host's decisions and findings either both changed or both as they were. The hourly sweep is not an evaluation run, so the watch job does not see it: each slice of 100 hosts commits its ends, audit entries, and re-triage together, and a sweep killed part way leaves the rest for the next hour, which finds them again from the stored data. A control past its last day is out of force meanwhile anyway (the control source reads the date).
- **Audit**, target the host (so the host page's audit section and the host filter show them): `vulnerability.decision_recorded` (advisory, kind, reason, until, bucket before and after for a control, the evidence in words, confirmed for), `decision_changed` (reason and until, before and after), `decision_ended` (by the operator, or by the system when fixed or resolved), `decision_expired`, and `decision_suspended` (what got worse: "known exploited: no, now yes"). The audit log escapes every value.
- **Host tab** (VT-D04's decision links). Needs attention gains a Decision column: "Record a control…" and "Accept the risk…" (as drawn), or the decision in force ("Risk accepted until …, not due"), one past its last day, a suspended one with what got worse and "Record a new decision…", or how the last one ended. O1's reason reads "<operator> recorded on <date>, until <date>: “<reason>” Without it: <bucket> (<rule>).", with "Change or end". A decision on a Low risk finding (an acceptance, or a control no longer in force) shows its tag and "Change or end" on the finding's nested row, and a decision due whose advisory has no finding in Needs attention is listed under Needs attention ("Decisions due on findings outside Needs attention"), so every decision counted as due has a row. Decisions due (`HostDecisions`, one query for the host): the Needs attention advisories with no decision in force, plus decisions in force that end within 7 days, active ones past their last day, and suspended ones, while the host still has an open finding of their advisory. The tab costs 2 more statements (the host's decisions; the controls read with the page's triage inputs), the same for 1 finding or 500.
- **A stored O1 after the last day.** The control source stops a control on the day after its last day, so every computed rule and reason no longer applies it ("When triaged: ... Re-triage pending"). The stored bucket, the summary counts, and the Low risk section keep O1 until the hourly job ends the decision and re-triages: up to about an hour after midnight UTC, plus the sweep's own run time to reach that host (minutes normally; up to about 50 minutes at 500,000 due, so up to about 2 hours), or longer if the queue is down. Meanwhile the decision is counted as due and listed with "Decision expired".
- **Not built here:** the Risk page's fleet-wide "Decisions due" (VT-RISK-PAGE, #263) and the summaries' `vuln_attention_undecided` (VT-SCORES, #262, built in parallel): counting accepted risks on the summaries needs the decision on the finding row or a join the summary triggers do not have; follow-up #298. Points on the summaries are VT-SCORES' too; the saved notice states the advisory's own points from the epic's table (`FindingDecisions::POINTS`).
- **Measured** (`bin/benchmark jobs --only decisions`, seed `--hosts 2000 --package-hosts 100 --advisories-per-source 5`, 4,450 open findings a package host; 2 shared CPUs, PostgreSQL 16 defaults; medians of 3 to 5 runs). The decision statements a host's transitions add: 2 a batch of up to 100 hosts with no decision (the batch's active decisions, and the controls in force), 0.9 to 1.1 ms a batch, about 0.01 ms a host; with a control on every host, 5 statements and 2.3 to 2.8 ms a batch (0.02 to 0.03 ms a host), against about 60 to 100 ms a host for the transitions themselves. The hourly sweep reads active decisions at 0.023 ms each (10,000 in 150 to 370 ms). At **1,000,000 active decisions with 500,000 due** (2,000 hosts by 500 advisories, half past their last day; review of #299): selecting them takes 12.5 to 16 s and about 150 MB, in 6,000 slices of at most 100 hosts and 100 due decisions; each slice is one transaction of 0.3 to 0.7 s, about 4 to 7 ms a decision ended (its audit entry and the re-triage), so about 40 minutes for the 500,000. The job runs one at a time (`limits_concurrency`, `on_conflict: :discard`): a run that would overlap a long one is dropped, since the next finds everything due again from the stored data. Selecting 500,000 due decisions grows the worker's memory by about 150 to 360 MB (peak about 470 MB, measured by the re-review), mostly garbage from reading every active row's evidence; fine for a worker. The re-triage a decision runs is about 1 to 6 ms a host. The form's candidates for an advisory every host has: 50 hosts in 40 to 60 ms, from at most 392 finding rows, whatever the fleet size. The tab: 2 more statements, the same for 1 finding or 500.
- **axe-core 4.14** (WCAG 2.1 A and AA, best practices) reports no violations on the form, the form with errors, the tab with a control, an acceptance, and a suspended decision, `?rule=O1`, a decision's page, and the audit log.
